===== Block A — What stays =====

* Gyroscope tilt = only move. Same practice as the climb. '''No''' jump button, '''no''' shoot button UI.
* Mage on the floor of the arena (under the marco divider).
* Orb is the weapon. Encanto still powers the orb (more damage / easier patch cadence — not a second game).
* In-arena platforms / jump / ''.mwr1'' stay '''out'''.
* Aviso + flag ''shazan'' + three languages + floor-only marco layout (art) stay.

===== Block B — Tap to shoot (all bosses) =====

Wife playtest: she tapped the glass when she wanted to fire. That becomes the rule.

* '''No''' on-screen shoot button.
* '''No''' hold-to-spray. Finger down does '''not''' keep firing.
* '''No''' auto-fire (current continuous orb shots go away).
* One '''tap''' = one shot. Several taps = several shots. Missed taps do nothing extra.
* You shoot only when you are sure the weak point is open. The screen stays readable.

===== Block C — Weak point (replaces the moving red bar) =====

The sliding red bar is '''dead'''. You do not chase a bar left/right.

* Collecting the '''patch''' (pendrive, existing 2-frame art) opens a vulnerability in the boss system.
* The weak point appears in '''one of three slots, at random''': far left, center, far right (under the boss box / on the divider line — exact pixels when we build).
* Window: '''10 s''', then it shuts. Need another patch.
* While that 10 s stun/window is running, the patch '''does not spawn again'''.
* Without Oracle encanto: patch drops are '''rare'''.
* Super-orb / encanto: patch drops are '''more frequent'''.
* Old path (patch sitting on hit-box platforms) is gone with the platforms.

===== Block D — Drops from the boss box (all bosses) =====

Below the boss portrait, along the strip that splits boss box from arena, things '''fall''' into the floor zone.

* Mix of harmful drops and (at least) the '''patch''' as the good one.
* Fall speed: '''not slow'''. Fast enough that you must tilt to catch it or you lose it.
* Harmful drops: if they catch the mage, '''one heart'''.
* Unique extras per boss can ride this same rain (Phishing-Man hook is separate — Block E).

===== Block E — Phishing-Man hook (unique) =====

Kiron will supply two images: (1) the hook, (2) the cart that carries it. Cart sits on the '''divider strip''' between boss box and arena.

* The cart + '''retracted''' hook travel left/right for the whole fight (hook stays close to the cart).
* Every '''~5 s''' the cart '''stops''' wherever it happens to be (that stop is effectively random because of the patrol).
* Then the hook '''drops fast''' to the '''orb’s height''' and stays '''static''' there.
* If the mage/orb passes near that parked hook, it '''fishes the orb''' (not the mage). '''Cannot shoot for 5 s'''.
* Then the hook reels back to the cart and patrol resumes.

This is the special that replaced “hook you off a platform into a pit”.
