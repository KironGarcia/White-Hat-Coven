===== Block B — Hearts / first hit / 7-day card =====

'''What Kiron saw:''' with 2 hearts on screen, the first hit (fall, boss, anything from the start of the run) did nothing; the second hit removed a heart. After a record roulette that showed '''coins''', later runs had '''3''' hearts.

'''What was going on:'''

* The 7-day card sets ''vidasMaximas()'' to 3. The run HUD drew only filled hearts and sat under a full-width altitude label, so a third heart could look like “still 2”. First hit 3→2 looked like no damage.
* Roulette: vitrine spins all prizes; the drop can be the card while the last frames still look like coins. Card was credited anyway (''concederCarta7Dias'').
* Boss virus used '''hat-only''' hitbox — body graze felt like a hit and did not take a heart.
* Mini-zombie stomp after ''resolverPouso'' (phase 4 only) could skip the first stomp. Real bug, but '''not''' Kiron’s “from the start of the run” report.

'''Locked now:'''

* HUD: ''vidasMaximas()'' slots (full / dim empty); hearts above safe area; altitude inset so it does not cover hearts (''HUD.tsx'').
* Boss hearts: same slot logic (''CoracoesArena'').
* Virus vs mage: full player hitbox (''verificarHitVirus'').
* Roulette: prize on screen = prize granted; larger result line; one-shot deliver.
* One-time wipe: ''corrigirCartaFantasmaUmaVez'' + ''CHAVE_RESET_CARTA_FANTASMA'' — first boot of this build clears a phantom card so testers start at 2 hearts.

Do '''not''' cap the HUD at 2 when the card is active. Do '''not''' shrink virus collision back to hat-only.

==== F1 — What went wrong (do not repeat) ====

* Adding '''13 mm after fit''' made the mage taller than its slot → hat into balloon, buttons on the legs. Worse on the tester APK (hidden status bar, different insets / display size).
* Growing only the '''flex hole''' does nothing when scale is already capped at '''2.8''' (width/height already larger than the cap).
* Collapsing balloon ''flex: 1'' / ''flexGrow: 0'' makes a thin strip and tiny type (font shrinks to the measured hole). '''Never do that again.'''

==== F2 — Signed numbers (mm via ''PX_POR_MM'' = 160/25.4) ====

| Constant | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| ''EXTRA_HUECO_MAGO_PX'' | '''13 mm''' | Added to sprite height '''and''' mage strip ''flexBasis'' / ''minHeight'' |
| ''APROXIMA_BALAO_PX'' | '''4 mm''' | Subtracted from balloon ''paddingBottom'' (''sobeBalao'') |
| ''APROXIMA_BOTOES_PX'' | '''3 mm''' | ''marginTop'' negative on the button strip |
| Scale cap | '''2.8''' | ''min(widthFit, heightFit, 2.8)'' then '''+ 13 mm''' |
| Mage↔buttons gap | '''¼ of leftover''' | ''justifyContent: flex-end'' + ''paddingBottom = (slotH − mageH) / 4'' (half of the old centered gap) |
| Balloon type | ''fonteDoBalao'' as fitted | Start ''innerH/1.05'', cap 24, line-height ~1.15×. '''Do not subtract 2 pt after fit''' (that left empty air in the balloon) |

''sobeMago'' / ''sobeBalao'' (14 mm / 10 mm max, proportional to ''marcoH'') stay. Balloon keeps ''flex: 1'' inside ''faixaBalao''.

==== F3 — Layout rule ====

The balloon size is '''not''' the thing that “grew”. The mage overflowing the slot was. If the mage looks small, raise ''EXTRA_HUECO_MAGO_PX'' a few mm (sprite + hole together) or raise the 2.8 cap — do '''not''' kill balloon flex to make room.

If the hat overlaps the tail: increase ''APROXIMA_BALAO_PX'' a millimetre or reduce extra mage mm. Do not rewrite the flex column.
